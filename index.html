lunet-dav — NC31 WebDAV/OCS emulator

A local work-alike for selected NextCloud Enterprise 31 WebDAV and OCS surfaces, implemented with lunet, Lua, and PostgreSQL.

Chassis bootstrap routes are served under /api/. NC31-compatible flows are under /remote.php/dav/files/..., /ocs/v2.php/..., and /index.php/login/v2. A health check is available at /health.
